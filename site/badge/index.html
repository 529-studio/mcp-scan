What it means

Scanned, not assumed

The config, the tool manifest, and — where permitted — a sandboxed dynamic probe.

Dated, not perpetual

The listing shows the scan date. Re-scan is quarterly or after a major change.

Revocable, not bought

No payment for a pass. A new high/critical finding starts a 30-day fix window.

What it is not

  • Not an endorsement of the whole product.
  • Not a pentest or a guarantee that the server is safe.
  • Not a certification body or a compliance certificate.
  • Not a pay-to-play mark. The first 10 are free; payment never buys a pass.
  • Not a reason to skip human review.

FAQ

Is this a certification?

No. It is a limited, revocable scan result for a specific surface and date. It is not a compliance certificate.

Does it mean the server is safe?

No. It means the scanned configuration and tools passed the current rules. Rules are heuristic and can miss things.

Can we pay for a pass?

No. The first 10 vendors are free. Payment never buys a pass. A badge is not for sale.

What happens if we fail?

You get private findings and a 30-day fix window. You can re-apply after the fix.

Do you scan without permission?

No. Live and dynamic scans require explicit permission.
