02

03

04

Render, review every number, and publish.

Top mistakes to check first

  • Secrets in the config.
  • Unpinned packages and images.
  • Broad filesystem roots.
  • Wildcard binds.
  • Write access by default.
  • Instruction-like tool descriptions.
  • Unconstrained command/path/url fields.
  • Hidden content in descriptions.
  • False read-only annotations.
  • No audit trail.

Limitations

  • Small sample, not a representative study.
  • Static, live, and dynamic findings are not equivalent.
  • Rules are heuristic and can produce false positives.
  • No evidence, server names, or credentials are included.
