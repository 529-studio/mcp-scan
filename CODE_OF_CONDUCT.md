# Code of conduct

Be direct, be kind, assume good intent.

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Do not harass, dox, or personally attack anyone.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported to `contact@529studio.site`. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate this policy.

This is a small project. Keep it useful, keep it civil.
